The Challenge

Athletes Unlimited Softball is poised for its second season, and with that comes a critical opportunity: transforming casual viewers into passionate, loyal fans. The league faces the unique challenge of building engagement in an innovative format while competing with established professional leagues that benefit from decades of tradition and fanbase cultivation.

Our mission is to design a fan engagement framework that leverages the strengths of a traditional team-based league structure, where consistent rosters and long-term rivalries create the kind of emotional investment that drives sustained loyalty. We're tasked with identifying what makes fans connect deeply with sports organizations and translating those insights into actionable strategies tailored to Athletes Unlimited's distinctive model.

Our Approach

We're taking a multi-layered approach that combines data analysis with creative thinking. By examining digital fan behavior patterns, live-game experience design, and broader sports consumption trends, we're building a comprehensive understanding of what drives fan engagement in modern professional sports.

Our work involves exploring engagement benchmarks from other successful team-based leagues, identifying critical fan touchpoints that span both physical and digital experiences, and assessing which proven engagement models can be effectively adapted for Athletes Unlimited's second season structure.
