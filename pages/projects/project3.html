The Challenge

First Touch Soccer Academy, a growing youth soccer training organization in Austin, approached our team with a clear goal: stand out in an increasingly competitive market while creating a seamless digital experience for parents and players. With numerous soccer academies vying for attention in the Austin area, they needed more than just incremental improvements, they needed a complete strategic overhaul.

Our mission was twofold: elevate their market positioning through data-driven insights and modernize their entire digital presence to convert interest into enrollment. This meant diving deep into competitor analysis, reimagining their social media strategy, forging community partnerships, and rebuilding their website from the ground up.

Our Approach

We began with comprehensive market research, benchmarking First Touch against every major soccer academy in the Austin area. This wasn't just about comparing prices, we analyzed program structures, coaching philosophies, facility access, and brand positioning to identify gaps in the market that First Touch could uniquely fill.

Simultaneously, we conducted a full audit of their existing website, examining everything from navigation flow to SEO performance. Through Google Analytics deep dives and user experience testing, we identified friction points in the registration process and opportunities to optimize conversion rates.

Strategic Insight

Our research revealed that while competitors focused heavily on elite player development, there was significant demand for academies emphasizing holistic youth development and community integration, areas where First Touch naturally excelled but undermarketed.
