Inside the 2025 NIL Arms Race

Power Five collectives will cross the $1.2B threshold this school year. BOSSO analysts reverse engineered seven NIL programs, modeled their projected spend per athlete, and outlined how student-led organizations can augment collectives without burning out their members.

9 minute read NIL marketplace Benchmarks + dashboards

Three Forces Reshaping NIL Budgets

  • Shift to evergreen retainers. 63% of the dollars we tracked are moving from one-off car dealership activations to recurring retainers tied to deliverables (podcasts, signing events, club appearances).
  • Brand cash versus booster cash. For the first time, external brands exceed booster collectives at Texas peer schools—up to 54% of the spend in our sample.
  • Student-operator premium. Brands now earmark 8–12% of every activation budget toward operations talent. That is the wedge BOSSO uses to offer pro-bono execution.

The BOSSO Deal Desk

We rebuilt our internal Airtable with inputs from collectives, sports law clinics, and pro-team partnerships. Each opportunity rides through four checkpoints:

  1. Signal capture. Scrape licensing filings, monitor athlete IG traction, and capture inbound sponsor asks.

  3. Activation scripting. Creative pod drafts run-of-show artifacts and content boards.
  4. Post-game report. We ship a recap deck within 72 hours that includes sentiment scores, booth traffic, and new-to-file fan counts.

Where We Go Next

Our recommendation to UT partners is simple: treat NIL like product development.

  • Prototype faster. Keep 20% of the budget flexible so athletes can join in-progress pilots without killing the semester plan.
  • Institute a design partner program. We’re onboarding three athletes as “insight scouts” who get first look at our dashboards.
  • Expand BOSSO’s deal review council. Alumni GMs now sit in on our Monday night reviews to train analysts on negotiation pressure.
