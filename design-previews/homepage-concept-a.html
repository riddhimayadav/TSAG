Vision Statement

Become the premier student organization for the business of sports at UT Austin and beyond, uniting students across multiple undergraduate programs and disciplines through a shared passion for sports, data, and innovation. Build a close-knit network of relationships with professional, collegiate, and industry sports organizations while creating meaningful pathways for students to gain real-world experience, develop lasting connections, and make an impact across the sports industry. Ultimately, establish BOSSO as a recognized pipeline of talent, innovation, and leadership for the future of sports business.

How We Work

Five pillars.
One organization.

The hierarchy makes each pillar easy to scan while giving Education enough room for both of its programs.

01

Consulting

Partner with real sports organizations to solve challenges across every corner of the sports industry. Turn data into impact, both on and off the field.

02

Research

Dive into player efficiency, team metrics, and the data behind the game. Build insights that fuel portfolios, research, and real-world results.

03

Education

Workshops

Master the tools of the trade through comprehensive technical workshops covering analytics software, statistical modeling, and data visualization. Complement your technical skills by building career readiness through workshops that cover networking, resume support, career strategy, and alumni connections that prepare members for internships and full-time roles.

BOSS Leaders Lectures

Gain exclusive access to industry leaders through our BOSS Leaders lecture series. Learn directly from executives, analysts, and innovators who are shaping the future of sports business, as they share insights, experiences, and strategic wisdom from the frontlines of the industry.

04

Media

Amplify the voices of sports analytics through podcasts, newsletters, articles, and social media. Uncover the insights and stories shaping the business of sports and share them across multiple platforms, building a community where data meets storytelling.

05

Philanthropy

Drive meaningful community impact through service initiatives and partnerships that reflect BOSSO’s values both on and off campus. Extend BOSSO’s reach throughout the greater Austin area by creating pathways for students to use sports as a vehicle for mentorship, inclusion, and positive social change.
