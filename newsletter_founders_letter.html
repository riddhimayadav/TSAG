Founder’s Letter · A New Season for BOSSO

Every August we pull the curtain back on the operating plan for BOSSO. The past year taught us that pro-level results arrive when we treat this student organization like a studio. So heading into 2025-26 we are doubling down on three commitments.

“Clubs don’t get better by accident. They get better when we document the small systems that make the work feel inevitable.” — Declan Price, Founder

1. Build Projects That Matter

Every partner engagement must touch a revenue, fan, or community KPI. That means fewer hypotheticals and more co-building alongside actual industry leaders. We are launching a due diligence checklist that flags whether a brief wields impact for the partner and growth for our members.

2. Teach Craft, Not Just Tools

We are evolving onboarding from a one-time bootcamp into a studio apprenticeship. Analysts rotate through storytelling reviews, operations pods shadow pro crews, and each pod hosts a “mistake debrief” monthly.

Craft Reviews Weekly tear-downs of decks, dashboards, and meetings so members see how executives process their work.
Process Vault Centralized Notion hub capturing everything from meeting agendas to NIL negotiation scripts.

3. Invest in People

Our retention target is 85%. To get there, we launched a membership concierge team, embedded wellness office hours, and created stipends for students covering travel or software costs. Every member deserves to stay in the game.

Declan Price Founder & Executive Chair, BOSSO
declan.price@utexas.edu
