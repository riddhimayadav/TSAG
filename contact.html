WHY PARTNER WITH US

Partnering with the Business of Sports Student Organization connects your organization to a talented pipeline of students trained in advanced analytics and business strategy. BOSSO's mission—delivering real-world consulting, impactful research, and industry-focused education—aligns with the growing demand for data-driven decision-making across all sectors of business. Partnering with BOSSO gives your business direct access to top student talent, innovative data-driven insights, and unique brand exposure within a passionate and growing sports tech community.

WAYS TO MAKE AN IMPACT

BOSSO drives impact by equipping students with hands-on skills through consulting projects, research, and weekly workshops. Key events—case competitions, speaker series, and networking—connect students with industry leaders. Your partnership fuels this mission—expanding access to real-world experiences, elevating student engagement with industry, and shaping the next generation of leaders in sports analytics and beyond. Together, we can create opportunities that ripple across campus, inspiring collaboration, advancing research, and shaping the future of sports analytics.
